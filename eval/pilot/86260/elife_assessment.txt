In a novel and important approach, the authors have generated coronary artery endothelial cells from thrombectomy specimens of patients with acute myocardial infarctions (AMIECs). Compared to control healthy coronary artery endothelial cells (HCAECs) they display solid evidence for impaired growth, migration and tubulogenesis and major metabolic differences. This advances the field and provides opportunities for future studies including investigating new avenues of therapy for AMI.
